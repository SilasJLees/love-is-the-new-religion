The Novel

Love Is The New Religion

A special-forces thriller. A spiritual awakening. One journey from war to unconditional love.

Synopsis

Commander James Wedmore leads the Black Widows, an elite unit that officially doesn't exist. But a mission deep in enemy territory sets him on a path no training could have prepared him for — one that leads not to another war, but to the discovery of a truth hidden within himself.

As Wedmore's journey unfolds alongside Sana and a cast of soldiers, seekers, and unlikely allies, Love Is The New Religion asks a question few thrillers dare to: what happens when the world's most feared people are shown a different way?
