Meet Silas

From Three Months in Bed to a Published Novelist

In January 2015, Silas J. Lees stopped getting out of bed. Three businesses had closed. What followed was a breakdown, a slow and honest recovery, and — nearly three years later — a 116,000-word novel he wasn't sure he had the words in him to write.
